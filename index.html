YouTube Clipper

A personal Mac app that turns long gaming videos into vertical Shorts and posts them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Finds the best moments in a long video, cuts them into 9:16 clips and adds captions, all on the owner's Mac.
  • Uploads the clips to the owner's YouTube channel as private videos, then posts the ones the owner approves on a schedule.
  • Reads view counts for those clips so the owner can see which ones did well.

Who it is for

It is a private tool used by one person on one channel. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Google account access

The app asks for YouTube permissions only so it can upload, schedule and manage videos on the owner's own channel and read that channel's statistics. See the privacy policy.
